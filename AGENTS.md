This repository is the canonical Stage 1 valuation and commercial-evidence
lineage. Treat `memory/venture/`, promoted `bundles/`, and explicitly reviewed
state records as authoritative Git-governed evidence. Do not let readiness,
automation, deployment, or internal coherence imply external validation.
Promotion and decisions remain explicit and evidence-cited. Run `make check`
before committing.

Read `CURRENT_STATE.md`, `memory/mission.md`,
`memory/profiles/valuation.md`, the pinned paths in `skillfoundry.toml`, and
`docs/architecture.md`.
